ABOUT ELENZ (100 words)

Elenz (elenz.co.nz) is a free, open tracker of New Zealand's electricity system. It shows
what is generating right now, updated every five minutes, alongside wholesale prices, demand
and emissions, for New Zealand and each island. Its history goes back to 1997, so today's
figures can be read against nearly three decades of data, and a Records page flags new highs
and lows as they happen. Everything is built on the Electricity Authority's public data, and
the method behind every estimate is published on the site. Charts and data are free to reuse
with credit. Elenz has no ads and is not affiliated with any organisation.


SHORT VERSION (50 words)

Elenz (elenz.co.nz) is a free, open tracker of New Zealand's electricity: generation, prices,
demand and emissions every five minutes, for each island, with history and records back to
1997. It is built on Electricity Authority data, with every method published. Charts are free
to reuse with credit. No ads, no affiliations.

KEY FACTS

- Live data: generation by fuel and plant, wholesale prices, demand, emissions and the HVDC transfer (live HVDC is estimated),
  every 5 minutes, for New Zealand, the North Island and the South Island.
- History: every half-hour since 1 August 1997.
- Records: highest and lowest half-hours, days, weeks, months and years. Recent records are
  "provisional" until the Electricity Authority's metered data (5-6 weeks after each month)
  confirms them.
- Estimates: embedded (rooftop and small) solar is modelled from satellite sunshine data and
  labelled as an estimate wherever it appears.
- Data: the Electricity Authority's Electricity Market Information (EMI), CC BY 4.0.
  Methods for every figure: https://elenz.co.nz/methods
- Open: code on GitHub (github.com/elenz-nz/elenz, MIT licence).
- Free, no ads, not affiliated with any organisation, generator, retailer or agency.
